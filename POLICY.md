# Project Policy

## Purpose

This document records repository-level policy for `rag_builder`.

## Authority

STN-LABZ organizational policy governs this project where applicable.

Project-specific behavior will remain subordinate to applicable organizational policy.

## Human Authority

`rag_builder` does not approve source material or establish organizational truth.

Human-approved source material remains authoritative.

## Source Integrity

The application will preserve traceability from generated knowledge back to its source.

Generated content will not silently replace, reinterpret, or modify authoritative source documents.

## Source Modification

Source directories used for ingestion will be treated as read-only unless a future authorized workflow explicitly defines otherwise.

## Trust

Trust will not be inferred solely from file presence, filename, location, or retrieval similarity.

Applicable approval, provenance, integrity, and Trust Chain requirements must be satisfied before generated knowledge is accepted as trusted.

## Modularity

Specialized processing behavior will be implemented in modules with defined interfaces.

New modules are subject to the applicable STN-LABZ module creation and qualification process.

## Failure Behavior

Malformed, unsupported, unapproved, or integrity-invalid input will be rejected or quarantined according to implemented policy.

The application will not silently convert a validation failure into success.
